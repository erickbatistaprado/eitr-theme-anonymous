# Anonymous artwork

The 3 wallpapers are art inspired by the Guy Fawkes mask worn by Anonymous, shared here as fan content for
personal desktops, free of charge. Each picture belongs to its artist, and the work it
shows belongs to their photographers and Warner Bros. (the mask design from V for Vendetta). This theme is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

- `01-hooded-mask.jpg`: A hooded figure in a Guy Fawkes mask; artist unknown
- `02-neon-mask.jpg`: A hooded figure in a neon mask; artist unknown
- `03-the-mask.jpg`: The Guy Fawkes mask in the dark; artist unknown

If one of these is yours, open an issue to be credited by name or to have it removed.

The palette is drawn from these pictures: black background, pale grey text, neon red accent, mask red, signal green, amber, smoke blue, violet and cyan.
